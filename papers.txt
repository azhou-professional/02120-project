Constellation parameters (used in web/sim/presets.py)
=====================================================

Checked on 2026-10-31. "Verified" = read from the source itself; "reported" = seen quoted in a
search result but the document could not be opened from here.

4. Galileo. European GNSS Service Centre, "Reference Constellation Orbital and Technical Parameters".
   https://gsc-europa.eu/node/502
   Verified: 24 nominal satellites, Walker 24/3/1, 3 planes of 8, semi-major axis 29,599.8 km,
   eccentricity 0, inclination 56.0 deg, planes 120 deg apart, 45 deg spacing inside a plane, 15 deg
   between adjacent planes. (Its RAAN values refer to an epoch of 2016-11-21; the app uses an
   arbitrary orientation instead.)
   Repeat cycle: 17 revolutions in 10 days, ESA Navipedia, https://gssc.esa.int/navipedia/index.php/GALILEO_Architecture

5. BeiDou-3. China Satellite Navigation Office, "BeiDou Navigation Satellite System Signal In Space
   Interface Control Document, Open Service Signal B2b (Version 1.0)", BDS-SIS-ICD-B2b-1.0, July 2020.
   Local file: paper/beidou.pdf
   Verified (section 3.1 "Space Constellation", PDF page 11): 3 GEO + 3 IGSO + 24 MEO; GEO at 35,786 km
   altitude, at 80, 110.5 and 140 deg E; IGSO at 35,786 km, inclination 55 deg; MEO at 21,528 km altitude,
   inclination 55 deg. (The app's MEO semi-major axis 27,906.1 km = 6,378.137 + 21,528 km.)
   The 2020 general SIS ICD (http://en.beidou.gov.cn/SYSTEMS/ICD/202008/P020200803539206360377.pdf, could
   not be opened from here) is reported to give reference semi-major axes 27,906.1 km and 42,162.2 km.
   MEO Walker 24/3/1 and the 12 h 53 min (7/13 sidereal day) period: Januszewski (2016),
   https://transnav.eu/html,658.html (verified: "selected from the Walker 24/3/1 constellation"), and
   https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/BeiDou (verified: 7/13 sidereal day, 7 sidereal day revisit period).
   IGSO ground-track crossing at 118 deg E: ESA Navipedia, https://gssc.esa.int/navipedia/index.php/BeiDou_Space_Segment
   and Januszewski (2016) (verified; both describe the planned final constellation). Later IGSO launches
   are listed at 120 deg E on Wikipedia; not used.
   Not verified: the exact IGSO phasing between the three satellites (the app makes their ground tracks
   coincide, an idealization).


Data
====

6. Natural Earth, 1:110m Physical Vectors, "Land" (polygons of the world's land masses).
   Used only to colour the low-poly Earth (land vs ocean); not used in any DOP calculation.
   Local file:   data/ne_110m_land.geojson   (127 polygons, downloaded 2026-10-31)
   Downloaded from: https://raw.githubusercontent.com/nvkelso/natural-earth-vector/master/geojson/ne_110m_land.geojson
   Dataset page: https://www.naturalearthdata.com/downloads/110m-physical-vectors/110m-land/
   Terms:        https://www.naturalearthdata.com/about/terms-of-use/
                 ("All versions of Natural Earth raster + vector map data ... are in the public
                 domain"; attribution is not required, but optional: "Made with Natural Earth.")
   Turned into web/data/land_mask.json (1-degree grid) by tools/landmask.py.


7. GPS almanacs (YUMA format) for GPS weeks 1003-1016 (28 March - 3 July 1999), the almanacs the FAA
   report (item 2) used for its Table 2-1.
   Local files:     web/data/almanac/1999/almanac.yuma.week<N>.<toa>.txt (14 files, downloaded 2026-10-31)
   Downloaded from: https://celestrak.org/GPS/almanac/Yuma/1999/ (CelesTrak's archive of the US Coast
                    Guard Navigation Center's weekly almanacs)
   Used by web/sim/almanac.py and tools/reproduce_faa.py (results in web/data/faa_reproduction.json).
